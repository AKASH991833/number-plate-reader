Honest limits: this is general-purpose OCR tuned for plates, not a traffic-camera ANPR system. Clean, straight, close-up photos read accurately; blurry, angled, dirty or stylized plates can misread — always sanity-check the result. It doesn't detect or crop the plate automatically, doesn't identify owners, and works for lawful personal use only.
